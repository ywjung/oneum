# OnEum User Guide (iPhone)

## Adding music
OnEum plays the music that is in its own folder. There are three ways to put music there.

- **In the Files app**: move or copy music folders or files into **Browse → On My iPhone → OnEum**.
- **From a Mac**: connect the iPhone with a cable, choose it in Finder, and drag music folders onto **OnEum** in the **Files** tab.
- **In OnEum**: **Settings → Library → Import Files** (or **Import Folder**) copies music from elsewhere, such as iCloud Drive, into OnEum's folder. A folder in iCloud Drive has to have its files downloaded to the iPhone first.

- Music that was added appears when OnEum is opened again. To read it at once, press **Settings → Library → Rescan**.
- A music file in another app can also be opened with **Share → OnEum**.
- OnEum only reads music files. It never changes or deletes files or their tags. Favourites, play counts and playlists are kept inside OnEum only.
- No account and no internet connection are needed.

## The screen
- **Tabs at the bottom**: Library · Search · Playlists · Settings. Pressing the open tab again returns to its first page.
- **Library**: Songs · Albums · Artists · Genres · Favorites · Listen Again · Folders · Statistics.
- **‹** at the top left goes back to the page before.
- **The bar above the tabs**: the song that is playing. Press it to open the player on the whole screen.

## Listening
- Pressing a song in a list puts the whole list into the queue and plays from that song. **Play** and **Shuffle** above a list start from the top or in a shuffled order.
- **⋯** at the right of a song (or a long press on it): Play Next · Add to Queue · Add to Playlist · Favorite · Go to Album · Go to Artist · Song Info · Remove.
- **The player**: cover, title (press it for the album), heart (favourite), position, shuffle · previous · play · next · repeat, app volume. **⌄** at the top left closes it.
- **Up Next**: the list button at the top right of the player. Press a song to play from it; ⋯ removes it from the queue.
- **Sleep Timer** and **Stop After Current Song** are under ⋯ of the song that is playing.
- Playback goes on with the screen off and while another app is used. The lock screen, Control Center and the buttons of headphones play, pause and skip.
- A phone call pauses the music, and it goes on when the call is over. When headphones are pulled out the music pauses rather than coming out of the speaker.
- Loudness is set with the iPhone's volume buttons. **App volume** in the player turns down OnEum's own sound only.

## Search
- The **Search** tab finds by title, artist and album.
- Below the field, choose what to look for: **Songs · Albums · Artists**.
- A search can be narrowed to a field: `artist:beatles` · `album:…` · `genre:jazz` · `year:1990-1999` · `format:flac` · `fav:yes`.

## Playlists
- **＋** at the top right of the **Playlists** tab makes a new playlist. So does ⋯ → **Add to Playlist** on a song.
- Open a playlist and press **⋯** at the top right: Rename · Export as M3U8 · Delete.
- **Settings → Library → Import Playlist** reads an M3U8 file. A playlist that lies in OnEum's folder finds the music files beside it.

## Settings
- **Library**: where the music is, Rescan, importing files, folders and playlists.
- **Playback**: volume leveling (ReplayGain), crossfeed, radio, shuffle by album, resuming long tracks, memory playback.
- **Equalizer**: ten bands and presets. Swipe sideways to reach all bands.
- **General**: language and appearance (light · dark · system).

## What differs from the Mac and Windows versions
- The iPhone decides where the sound goes and at which sample rate. There is no choice of output device, no output at the song's sample rate and no exclusive output.
- There is no menu bar and there are no keyboard shortcuts, no Show in Finder, and nothing can be dragged onto a window.
- Not on the iPhone screens yet: the VU meter and spectrum, lyrics and credits, file check and loudness analysis, making smart playlists, reordering a playlist, selecting several songs at once, importing an Apple Music library.
